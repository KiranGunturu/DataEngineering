hive vertex error:

•	The actual issue here is the hive service uses hive-serde-3.1.3-amzn-0.jar that does not have the specific class (ColumnProjectionUtils) and method(appendReadColumns) in it. Due to this, we get the below error.
java.lang.NoSuchMethodError: org.apache.hadoop.hive.serde2.ColumnProjectionUtils.appendReadColumns(Lorg/apache/hadoop/conf/Configuration;Ljava/util/List;Ljava/util/List;Ljava/util/List;)V
•	Spark uses hive-serde-2.3.9-amzn-2.jar, which has the required class and method. That's you don't see the error in spark-sql CLI.
•	We pushed the different lower versions of the serde Jar files (hive-serde-2.3.9-amzn-2.jar, hive-serde-2.2.0.jar, and hive-serde-2.1.0.jar) to hive-related directories. However, we get the same "NoSuchMethodError" error. 
•	We have been actively working with the Support team. We will keep you posted.
