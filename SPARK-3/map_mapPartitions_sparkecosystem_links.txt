Map and Map Partition
======================

this rdd has 10000 rows and 10 partitions

each partition holds 1000 records

fun1

rdd.map(fun1) - this will be called 10000 times as map works on each row

rdd.mapPartitions(fun1) - this will be called 10 times as there are 10 partitions. mapPartitions works on each partition
