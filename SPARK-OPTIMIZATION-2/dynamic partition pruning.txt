dynamic partition pruning
==========================

1. predicate push down
2. partition pruning

predicate pushdown doesnot help unless our data is partitioned on filter columns

lets say if we have date dimension table data and we are joining with orders fact table
but in our query we are applying filters on non partitioned columns

orders-fact
--------------
order_id,order_date,prod_id,unit_price,qty

dimension-dates
---------------

full_date,year,month,day


select year,month,sum(unit_price * qty) as total_sales
from orders
join dates on order_date = full_date
group by year, month
where year='2021' and month='02'

now do two things to avoid SMB here

1. enable dynamic partition pruning feature
2. apply broadcast on the dimension table

works for fact and dimension-like tables

fact table must be partitioned
broadcast the dimension table
