if you want to decrease the number of partiitions:
===================================================

repartition and coalesce

to decrease the number of partitions coalesce is preferred as it will try to minimize the shuffling.


N1		P1, P2,	P3,	P4


N2		P5, P6,	P7,	P8


N3		P9, P10, p11, p12


N4		P13, P14,	P15,	P16

rdd has 16 partitions

rdd1.repartition(8) - it has intention to have final partiitions of exatly equal size and for this it has to go through complete shuffling.

rdd1.coalesce(8)

N1		P1, P2 (p3 and p4 got merged here itself)


N2		P5, P6 (p7 and p8 got merged here itself)


N3		P9, P10 (p11 and p12 got merged here itself)


N4		P13, P14 (p15 and p16 got merged here itself)

coalesce has a intention to minimize the shuffling and combines existing partiitions on each machine to avoid a full shuffle.
