2. Stages in spark

stages are marked by shuffle boundaries

whenever we encounter a shuffle, a new stage gets created

whenever we call a wide tranformation a new stage gets created.

if we use 3 wide tranformations , we will 3+1 stages. by default we will have 1 stage for tranformations.


if we have 2 stages ...

and stage 2 reads it back from disk.
