Broadcast join
==================

1. simple - shuffle
2. broadcase - this does not require a shuffle

whenever we are joining 2 large dataframes then it will invoke a simple join and shuffle will be required


when we have one large dataframe and the other dataframe is smaller. in that case we can go for broadcast join.

in this case small dataframe data will be sent to each executor.

what if both the dataframes are pretty small - then dont use spark as spark is mainly used for big data.
