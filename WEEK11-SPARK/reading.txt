there are 3 read modes
======================

1. PERMISSIVE (default) - it sets all the fields to null when it encounters a corrupted record and creates a new column called
	_corrupt_record
	
2. DROPMALFORMED - will ignore the malformed error
3. FAILFAST - whenever a malformed record is encounterd then an exception is raised.
